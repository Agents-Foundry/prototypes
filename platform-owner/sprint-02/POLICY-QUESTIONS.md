# Sprint 02 — Product-policy decisions awaiting individual review

**No policy below is automatically approved by inclusion in the repository.** Product-owner review, independent security governance and final visual sign-off are distinct.

| ID | Decision | Proposed design default | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| PLT-DEC-01 | Platform vs tenant admin privilege boundary | **APPROVED — Option B:** Strict cross-tenant isolation and deny-by-default platform operator access to tenant conversations, credentials, secrets and internal content. Operators may manage tenant metadata, lifecycle, subscriptions and platform operations. Exceptional support access requires customer authorization, explicit scope, time limits, stronger approval/independent oversight, immutable audit and immediate revocation; routine operator views remain redacted. | Product owner approved 2026-10-16; independent security review pending |
| PLT-DEC-02 | Tenant creation authority | **APPROVED — Option B:** Tenant creation restricted to authorized platform staff; validation of uniqueness, domain ownership where applicable, region, plan, quotas, and security defaults. Standard requests follow risk-based policy; high-risk, costly, or privileged provisioning requires four-eyes approval without self-approval. Lifecycle: Draft → Review → Pending Approval (if required) → Provisioning → Ready for Admin Setup/Active only after authoritative success. Provisioning must be idempotent, audited, safely retryable/rollback-capable; failed or partial operations do not claim activation. Initial admin account issued only after identity/provisioning checks. Trial/sandbox tenants retain isolation and resource limits. | Product owner approved 2026-10-16; independent security review pending |
| PLT-DEC-03 | Region and residency | **APPROVED — Option B:** Tenant selects an actually supported deployment region during provisioning. Residency scope explicitly covers databases, files, conversations, logs, backups, runtime workspaces, telemetry and recovery copies; storage location alone does not guarantee processing residency. Model providers/routing and connectors must comply with tenant regional transfer controls. No silent relocation. Region changes require an authorized migration request, impact assessment, appropriate customer approval, validation, backup/rollback/recovery planning, audit trail and authoritative completion verification; unsupported capabilities must be disclosed. | Product owner approved 2026-10-16; independent security review pending |
| PLT-DEC-04 | Plans and billing | **APPROVED — Option B:** Central versioned subscription catalog with policy-controlled per-tenant feature entitlements and quotas; separately attribute subscriptions, metered AI usage, organization-paid provider charges and permitted employee BYOK. Trials, upgrades, downgrades, renewals and cancellations have explicit effective dates and impact previews, without silent disruption of active AI employees. Warn at soft quotas and enforce hard quotas safely for in-flight work. Discounts, credits, exceptional entitlements and limit increases require authorized audited approval. Payment failure has explicit grace/restricted/suspended states without automatic tenant-data deletion. Distinguish estimated, reconciled and invoiced amounts; support disputes and corrections. | Product owner approved 2026-10-16; independent security review pending |
| PLT-DEC-05 | Support break-glass | **APPROVED — Option B:** Routine support requires explicit tenant authorization, purpose-bound least privilege and time-limited scopes. Exceptional emergency break-glass is restricted to defined critical incidents when ordinary authorization is unavailable or insufficient, requires documented incident justification, independent authorization/separation of duties and no self-approval. No automatic access to tenant secrets, conversations, unrestricted exports or customer-managed encrypted content. Sessions expire automatically, can be revoked immediately and log requests, decisions, resources, actions and timestamps immutably. Notify the affected tenant promptly; any justified notification delay must be independently reviewed. Mandate retrospective post-incident review and investigation of misuse. Technical and encryption boundaries cannot silently be bypassed. | Product owner approved 2026-10-16; independent security review pending |
| PLT-DEC-06 | Suspension/termination | **APPROVED — Option B:** Governed, reversible suspension with documented reason, authorization, appropriate tenant notice, audit and safe handling of in-flight AI work. Reactivation revalidates tenant identity, billing/security requirements, policies, integrations, credentials and approvals. Subscription cancellation is distinct from irreversible deletion. Termination/deletion requires retention and legal-hold review, customer communication, independent dual authorization without self-approval, explicit scope and impact confirmation, controlled progress, failure/retry states and verified deletion outcome. Backups, audit evidence and recovery eligibility follow applicable retention obligations; no promise of restoration once irreversibly deleted. All transitions preserve tenant isolation. | Product owner approved 2026-10-16; independent security review pending |
| PLT-DEC-07 | Blueprint catalog distribution | **APPROVED — Option B:** Publish immutable uniquely versioned blueprints with signed manifests, declared capabilities, dependencies and compatibility constraints. Publishing follows Draft → Validation → Security Review → Approved → Staged Rollout → Published with rejected, paused and deprecated branches. Organizations govern adoption, approved auto-updates and supported version pinning; updates cannot silently elevate permissions, connector/model scope or data-handling privileges and require policy reassessment. Rollouts start with internal/pilot cohorts and phase wider availability under health thresholds, supporting pause and audited rollback to compatible prior releases while preserving tenant data/configuration. Deprecations include notice, support windows and upgrade guidance. Audit publisher, approvals, digests, rollout scope, adoption, failures and rollback. | Product owner approved 2026-10-16; independent security review pending |
| PLT-DEC-08 | Global policy templates | **APPROVED — Option B:** Versioned, approved mandatory platform security baselines for tenant isolation, secrets, privileged actions, approvals and audit; tenants may add stronger controls or configure only explicitly delegated settings, never weaken baseline. Effective-policy preview and rule provenance explain final enforcement. Invalid or conflicting policy fails closed. Changes require impact analysis, staged rollout, approval, rollback and audit. No silent permission escalation or removal of tenant protections. Emergency policy changes retain mandatory boundaries and require elevated documented approval. | Product owner approved 2026-10-16; independent security review pending |
| PLT-DEC-09 | Platform operator MFA/SSO | **APPROVED — Option B:** Platform staff use an approved workforce enterprise SSO identity provider separate from customer-tenant identities; all operators require MFA, with phishing-resistant passkeys/FIDO2 preferred for privileged roles. Require recent step-up verification for destructive tenant actions, emergency access, policy publishing, sensitive financial overrides and role changes. Scope operator roles by least privilege (platform owner, provisioning, billing, security reviewer, support, auditor); prohibit self-elevation and require independent approval for high-risk grants. Enforce session inactivity expiry, revocation, access reviews and prompt offboarding. Emergency recovery requires independently authorized, restricted, alerted and audited procedures. Log sign-in challenges, privileged changes and revocations without revealing credentials. | Product owner approved 2026-10-16; independent security review pending |
| PLT-DEC-10 | Telemetry and privacy | Tenant-minimized aggregate statistics, redaction and bounded retention | Pending |
